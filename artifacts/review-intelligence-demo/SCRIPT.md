# Review Intelligence — Mumbai Central demo

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Show the owner of Mumbai Central how Review Intelligence could turn a review into an organized signal, an alert to examine, a manager-reviewed reply, and a concise owner summary.
- **Identity:** Preserve the approved App Demo Showcase style: light-studio fog `#EEF2F6`, faint dotted field, ink `#0F172A`, secondary slate `#64748B`, violet `#7C3AED`, and white browser-frame UI. Sora display type; Inter interface labels.
- **Hero:** A synthetic review card, explicitly identified as fictional, moves through a clearly labeled demo workspace scoped to Mumbai Central's Falls Church address.
- **Motion:** A sample review expands into two topic tags; a separate illustrative trend alert surfaces; the sample signal connects to fictional staff praise, a manager-reviewed response, and a weekly example memo.
- **Arc:** Targeted opener → product overview → one fictional review tagged → demo-only alert → fictional staff recognition → response draft with human approval → honest data-provenance pause → sample owner summary → restrained product close.
- **Payoff:** The busy interface resolves to one readable example summary, with the opening sample-review card carried into the loop.
- **Format/runtime:** 16:9; exactly 120 seconds, preserving the existing nine-scene pacing. Silent, with all explanations readable on screen.
- **Target/context:** Mumbai Central, 7505 VA-7, Falls Church, VA 22043. User supplied this target. The restaurant name and address identify the intended demo location only.
- **Truth boundary:** No review CSV or POS export was supplied. No Google review content was imported or used. The interface contains only fictional review wording, synthetic staff names, and illustrative chart values. No on-screen alert, theme, praise, or summary describes Mumbai Central's actual performance. Do not suggest real guest text, Yelp data, actual restaurant results, quantified business impact, or endorsement.
- **Brand inputs:** No logo/brand assets were supplied. Use the name as ordinary text; do not fabricate a logo.
